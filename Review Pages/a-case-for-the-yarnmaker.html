The Ainsley McGregor Mysteries: A Case for The Yarn Maker

Year: 2025

Director: Martin Wood

Reviewed: 08/18/2026

#: 4

Foul cinema, a journey into bland suburban madness aboard a ship captained by DJ Tanner. At their best hallmark movies (I know it's not hallmark, but it is just a little more Christian) are fun to put on and laugh at. My wife and I go through two or three Christmas movies a year, pointing out weird writing or acting, and it's good. I like it. I can't call the movies good, but they do fulfill a role.

"A Case for the Yarn Maker" does not even offer that. Not that there's nothing to talk about, no! Plot holes and bad writing abound. A character who believes they are in danger calls the police and provides clues in code rather than just telling them who they think the murderer is. Evidence is missed by police because it had rolled under the chair the murder victim was sitting in. A book club is provided the full details of a case while the family of the victim are explicitly kept in the dark.

The cinematography and so forth have the same look as every other hallmark movie. That's fine, I get it. It's a factory that makes mediocre movies, but I just feel like I should mention. It's uninteresting in the same way that multi-cam sitcoms are. The only thing of note is that the makeup department seemed to have been instructed to make all the men look as white as possible. Just corpses up there.

I couldn't even bring myself to enjoy mocking it. It was so careless. A copy of a copy of a mass manufactured sweet. An off brand twinkie. I could write a better movie than this. I should not feel like I could do a better job while watching a movie. I'm an idiot.
