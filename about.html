About

Joseph Zavilla is a writer, filmmaker, visual artist, woodworker, gardener, let’s player, comedian, actor, and musician. He lives in West Virginia with his wife and two cats.

“Deep in the ocean of our collective unconscious there is a cosmic whale who is representative of all that is magical and good. Through artistic practice, we are able to put up a kind of antenna which allows the cosmic whale speak to us. It is like using your car radio. A byproduct of this relationship is GOOD ART. I cannot choose to make GOOD ART. I do not know how. I can only refine my process and work until GOOD ART appears on the page in front of me.” -JZ
